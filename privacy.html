Privacy

Privacy information.

When you submit a project enquiry, James Clifford Carpentry receives the information you choose to provide, such as your name, company, email, phone number, project location and project details.

This information is used to understand and respond to your enquiry, discuss the work, arrange quoting or site assessment, and manage any resulting business communication. It is not sold or supplied to lead-generation networks or advertising lists.

Project enquiry submissions are processed through Formspree. Formspree stores form submissions and hosts its services using Amazon Web Services in the United States, so information submitted through the form may be processed or stored outside Australia.

The enquiry form also uses Cloudflare Turnstile to help prevent automated spam. Turnstile processes technical signals needed to distinguish legitimate visitors from automated traffic and does not access, store or transmit the contents of the project enquiry form.

This website does not currently use Google Analytics, Umami or another general-purpose visitor analytics platform. If analytics are introduced, this page will be updated to reflect how they are used.

You can ask about personal information you have provided, request a correction, or request deletion where appropriate by emailing admin@jamescliffordcarpentry.com.au .
